- Say that your work is **"built on Enthalix"**, **"based on Enthalix"** or

- Redistribute **unmodified** Enthalix releases under the Enthalix name.
- Refer to Enthalix by name in articles, papers, talks, courses and reviews.
- Describe yourself as offering Enthalix training, support or consulting, as
  long as you don't suggest Enthalix LLC endorses you.

## You may not, without written permission

- Use "Enthalix", or anything confusingly similar, in the name of your own
  product, project, company or domain.

  your fork and say it is based on Enthalix.
- Use the logo in a way that suggests Enthalix LLC endorses, sponsors or
  certifies your product.
- Use the name or logo on merchandise.
